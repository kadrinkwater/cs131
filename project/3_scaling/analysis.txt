Analysis: Proportion of Wikipedia Traffic Coming from Search

Google Search AI Overview timeline
May 14, 2024: Launched in US
May-Aug 2024: rollout to several more countries
Oct 28, 2024: Launched worldwide


When Google launched AI Overview, website owners worried that it would reduce click-through traffic. Has it done so? We can make a guess by looking at the number / proportion of Wikipedia page views that come from external search engines (prev='other-search').
